Thanks for the question.

The write up is strictly needed by May 1 if you are receiving awards. If not, you can spare it completely.

However, I'd suggest include it by 4/24 if you can for 2 reasons: (1) MLSys this year has multiple competitions coordinated across multiple sponsors. The dates are currently aligned, but there is a chance that the 5/1 day may move. (2) we run ablation studies to identify the most confusing part(s) of the competition, and may remove those from solution validation process to give all participants the benefits of doubt. Including a write up helps us with the ablation study. For example if everyone interprets the spec in on way, we may just promote that to be the valid interpretation, so that we are fair to the participants coming from the diverse backgrounds/fields. 
</comment>
