\section{Overview}
\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Overview}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The solver needs to determine:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item Operators to fuse
    \item Retention of output tensors
    \item Iteration order (snake ordering is optimal)
    \item Tile size
  \end{enumerate}
  \pause
  \item Retained tensor's lifetime is limited and fusion constraints are relaxed
\end{itemize}
\pause
\begin{tcolorbox}[title={Our overall strategy}]
  Retention is local, fusion is global; fuse the nodes first, then consider retention.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{frame}
